    Client file

    The client's story, their reasons and what brings them to a session, in their own words. A genogram and free spaces for what you observe.

    Session follow-up

    Log every meeting and see the journey: how the client evolves, what was worked on and what comes next.

    Calendar and reminders

    Appointments by day, week or month, with reminders and confirmation by link so every session starts calmly.

    Agreements and consents

    Optional: if you use them, the client signs on screen or from their phone, at their own pace and with their own signature.

    Reports and certificates

    Templates for attendance certificates or referrals to other practitioners, with a verification code.

    Your own style

    Change, hide or create sections and templates to reflect your method. Nothing is mandatory: only the client's name is required.

    Check-ins and process notes

    Simple wellbeing check-ins, the wheel of balance and free or handwritten notes for whatever you want to remember.

    Simple finances

    Set your rates, see which appointments are paid or pending, record payments and partial payments, issue receipts and check the summary of income and balances.

    Surveys and team support

    Optional satisfaction survey for your clients and limited access for whoever helps you with the calendar.

    The rhythm of a process

    From the first conversation to a conscious closing

    • Meeting

      Welcome the client, listen to their reason and, if you wish, record the working agreement.

    • Journey

      Log each session: what was felt, done and proposed.

    • Follow-up

      Observe progress over time and adjust the support with clarity.

    • Closing

      Conclude the process with a closing note and recommendations for what comes next.

    Frequently asked questions

    Questions with simple answers

    Is it a clinical tool?

    No. Alter-Karute supports wellbeing and health-improvement processes with alternative and complementary therapies, but it is not a clinical tool: it carries out no clinical processes and does not diagnose or treat illnesses.

    Do I have to fill in all the details?

    No. Only the client's name is required; consents, file sections and each session's fields are optional.

    Do I need technical knowledge?

    No. It runs in the browser, on a computer or phone, and comes with sample data so you can explore before starting with real clients.

    Can I adapt the templates to my method?

    Yes. The file and session sections can be changed, added, hidden or removed so they match the way you work.

    How are consents signed?

    On screen during the session, or remotely: the client receives a link and signs from their phone.

    How is the information protected?

    Content is stored encrypted, each account sees only its own clients and can enable two-step verification.

    Can I take a copy of a client's file?

    Yes. Each file can be downloaded in full, with its signed documents and attachments.
