Privacy policy and use of personal data

Alter-Karute · In force since November 1, 2026 · Replaces the version of October 27, 2026

English summary (Google API data)
Scope of the platform. Alter-Karute supports wellbeing and health-improvement processes with alternative and complementary therapies, but it is not a clinical tool: it does not diagnose or treat illnesses. If a client has a health condition, they should see a health professional.

1. Who is responsible

Alter-Karute is a platform for practitioners to keep their clients' files. It is operated by Juan Pablo Giraldo, in Colombia. Contact for privacy matters: alterkarute.systems@gmail.com.

Processing details:

  • Regarding the data of the practitioners' accounts (name, email, signature and profile), the platform operator is the controller of the processing.
  • Regarding the data of the clients, the controller is each practitioner who records them and obtains the client's authorization. The platform acts as processor: it stores and protects them on the practitioner's behalf and uses them for nothing else.

2. What data is processed

  • Practitioners: name, email, password (stored only as an Argon2 cryptographic hash, never as text), ID number, registration, practice details, handwritten signature and usage preferences. Also the data of their team accounts (name and email); the configuration of their sending email, including an app password, stored encrypted; and, if they enable two-step verification, their secret key, recovery codes and the devices they chose to remember (browser, system and date of last use).
  • Clients (recorded by the practitioner): identification and contact details, sociodemographic data, caregivers or representatives, consents and signatures, file (first session, sessions, scales, impressions, reports, closings and handwritten sheets), appointments and attached documents. It includes sensitive health-related data.
  • Process notes: the practitioner's working notebook (hypotheses, case formulation, supervision notes). They are not part of the file, do not appear in copies or reports, and team accounts cannot see them.
  • Technical data: a log of accesses and actions, without session content: date and time, user, type of action, record it refers to and IP address. Each practitioner can view the log for their account and team. In addition, the IP address is used temporarily to limit sign-in attempts and queries.
  • Cookies: only two, both necessary: the session cookie, to keep you signed in, and, if the person chooses “Remember this device” when verifying the code, one that avoids asking for it again in that browser. No advertising or analytics cookies are used. Some preferences (light or dark mode, calendar view) are stored in the browser itself.

3. What they are used for

  • To let each practitioner create, sign, consult and keep their clients' files, and deliver copies when the law requires.
  • To schedule appointments and, if the practitioner enables it, show them in their Google Calendar or publish them on a private calendar link (.ics) that can be subscribed to from Google Calendar or another application. That link contains the date, time and format of each appointment, and the client identification the practitioner chooses: their initials, their name or none.
  • To show each practitioner aggregated statistics of their own practice (impressions, formats, case status and survey results), without identifying clients.
  • To verify the authenticity of reports through their verification code. Each verification is recorded in the practitioner's log.
  • If the practitioner enables it and the client authorized it, to send the client, from time to time, a satisfaction survey about the service. Answering is voluntary; answers are stored without the client's name and the practitioner sees only aggregated results. From the same link the client can ask not to receive more surveys.
  • If the practitioner sends it, to let the client read and sign their consent remotely with a personal link. To open it, their ID number is requested and, as a record of the signature, the date and time, IP address and device type are stored.
  • If the practitioner enables it, to send the client a reminder of their appointment with a link to confirm or cancel it: by email, automatically, or by WhatsApp, when the practitioner sends it from their own phone. The reminder contains only the date, time, format and practitioner's name; never session information.
  • During the trial (beta) version, to receive the feedback that practitioners and their teams send about the app, with the part of the app they were in and, if the commenter attaches it, a screenshot, to fix errors. Please do not include client data, not even in screenshots; comments are stored encrypted and only the administrator sees them.
  • To protect the security of the platform and comply with legal obligations.

The data is not sold, not used for advertising, not transferred to third parties for commercial purposes and not used to train artificial intelligence models.

4. Google data (Google Calendar)

The Google Calendar connection is optional and each practitioner enables it with their own Google account.

  • Permission requested: only calendar.app.created. It allows creating a calendar called “Alter-Karute” and managing only the events in that calendar. The platform cannot see or modify the practitioner's other calendars or personal events.
  • What is sent to Google: the date, time, duration and format of each appointment, and the client identification the practitioner chooses: their initials, their name or none. Notes and session information are never sent.
  • What is received from Google: an access permission (token) and the ID of the calendar created. The token is stored encrypted and used only to keep that calendar in sync.
  • How to withdraw access: in the platform (Calendar → Google Calendar → Disconnect) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On disconnecting, the token is revoked and deleted. The “Alter-Karute” calendar remains in the practitioner's Google account, who can delete it whenever they wish.
Alter-Karute's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. How they are protected

  • Session content, process notes, signatures, attachments, Google permissions and email app passwords are stored encrypted on the server.
  • Only the data the system needs to work is left unencrypted: users' emails and names, each record's type, identifier and dates, and the log (which has no session content).
  • All communication travels encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Each practitioner sees only their own clients. Administrators manage accounts but cannot read other practitioners' files.
  • The practitioner can create accounts for their team (secretary or assistants). Those accounts see only clients' identification and contact details and the calendar; never session information. Every change to client details is recorded with the date, time and who made it.
  • Each account can enable two-step verification with an authenticator app. If the person chooses to remember a device, the code isn't requested again there as long as it is used at least once every 30 days; changing the password or choosing “Forget all devices” cancels it. The session closes by itself after a period of inactivity, and there is a temporary lockout after several failed sign-in attempts.
  • Signed documents cannot be modified or deleted; they only admit dated and timed addenda.
  • Backups of the database are made.

6. Who they are shared with

  • Infrastructure providers that make the service possible (for example, the server and the Cloudflare secure connection service). They only carry or host the encrypted information and are obliged to protect it.
  • Google, only with the appointment data described in point 4 and only if the practitioner connects their account.
  • Email provider of the practitioner (or the server's, if the practitioner doesn't set up their own), which carries the invitations, reminders, surveys and signing links sent by email.
  • Whoever has the private calendar link that the practitioner enabled, with the appointment data described in point 3. The practitioner can deactivate or change it whenever they wish.
  • Whoever receives a report: can see that report's content on the verification page, using the code printed on it.
  • Authorities, when a regulation or court order requires it.

7. How long they are kept

  • The file is kept as long as the practitioner keeps it on the platform; the data subject can ask the practitioner for a copy or its deletion.
  • The access log is kept as a security record.
  • Account data is kept while the account is active and for the time needed to meet legal obligations.
  • Google permissions are deleted when the account is disconnected.

8. Rights of data subjects

Data subjects may access, update and rectify their data, request proof of the authorization granted, be informed about the use of their data, revoke the authorization or request deletion when there is no legal duty to keep them, and file complaints with the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio.

  • Clients: exercise their rights before their practitioner, who is the controller of their data. The platform helps them respond, for example with the full copy of the file.
  • Practitioners and any other query: write to alterkarute.systems@gmail.com. Queries are answered within a maximum of 10 business days and claims within a maximum of 15 business days.

9. Minors

The platform is used by adult practitioners. Data of minor clients is recorded by their practitioner with the consent of their legal representatives and, where applicable, with the minor's assent.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this same page with its effective date and practitioners will be informed within the platform.

Alter-Karute is a follow-up tool for alternative and complementary therapists. It supports wellbeing and health with alternative therapies. It is operated by Juan Pablo Giraldo (Colombia). Contact: alterkarute.systems@gmail.com.
